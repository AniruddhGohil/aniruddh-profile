Why it's different

AEO vs Traditional SEO

Traditional SEO

  • Rank #1 on the results page
  • Optimise for clicks to your site
  • Target keywords with high search volume
  • Build backlinks for domain authority
  • Measure success by traffic volume

AI Search Optimisation (AEO)

  • Get cited inside AI-generated answers
  • Optimise for being the trusted source AI references
  • Target conversational, intent-based queries
  • Build entity authority across the web
  • Measure success by brand citations and qualified intent

Six pillars of AEO

🧠
Entity & Brand Optimisation

Structure your brand as a recognised entity in Google's Knowledge Graph. Consistent Schema.org markup, sameAs links, and entity signals that AI systems use to identify and cite you.

❓
FAQ & Conversational Content

Identify the exact questions your audience asks AI tools. Structure your content to directly answer them — the format AI Overviews extract and surface as citations.

📋
Structured Data Implementation

Full Schema.org audit and implementation — FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Person, and Product schemas that give AI systems structured signals about your expertise and content.

🔍
AI Citation Audit

Test your brand across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini to map where you're cited, where competitors appear instead, and what gaps to close.

🌐
Cross-Platform Brand Footprint

Strengthen your brand presence across LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, and industry publications — the sources AI models are trained on and frequently cite.

✍️
Topical Authority Content Plan

A structured content roadmap to build depth in your niche — the single biggest factor in whether AI consistently recommends your brand over generic sources.

How it works

The AEO process

01
AI Visibility Audit

Test your brand across every major AI search surface — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Bing Copilot. Map where you appear, where you're absent, and where competitors are being cited instead.

02
Entity & Schema Foundation

Audit and implement structured data across your site. Establish your brand as a named entity with consistent signals — name, expertise, authorship, and cross-platform sameAs links that AI systems trust.

03
Conversational Content Restructure

Identify the high-intent questions driving AI queries in your category. Reformat or create content that answers directly — the specific structure that AI Overviews extract and surface as citations.

04
Ongoing Citation Monitoring

Monthly checks across AI platforms to track brand citation frequency, new gaps, and competitor movement. AEO is a continuous signal — not a one-time fix.
