for a Fragrance Brand

AI-driven content production, entity SEO for luxury fragrance categories, and structured data expansion — all executed within a 3-month engagement — delivering measurable organic growth for one of the UAE's most recognised fragrance brands.

+20% Organic Growth in 3 Months
3 mo Time to Measurable Results
UAE Market Luxury Fragrance
The Challenge

A beloved fragrance brand underperforming in organic search.

Rasasi had strong brand recognition and a loyal customer base in the UAE, but their organic search presence didn't reflect their market position. With a short 3-month engagement window, every decision had to generate fast, measurable impact.

🌸
Weak Category-Level Authority

Luxury fragrance categories — oud, oriental, floral — had no content depth or entity signals. Pages were product grids without the topical substance needed to rank competitively for category-level queries.

🏷️
Missing Structured Data

Product pages had no schema markup — meaning no rich results, no AI search citations, and no price/availability signals in SERPs for a brand competing in a high-value purchase category.

⏱️
Short Engagement Window

With only 3 months to deliver results, the strategy had to prioritise ruthlessly — focusing on the highest-leverage interventions that could show measurable organic improvement within the engagement timeline.

🔍
Generic Product Descriptions

Product descriptions were manufacturer copy — duplicated across multiple retailers and carrying no unique entity signals, no fragrance note structure, and no buyer-intent language.

The Approach

High-velocity SEO — maximum impact in minimum time.

With 3 months to show results, the approach prioritised ruthlessly: entity SEO for categories first, structured data for rich results second, AI content to scale third.

🗺️
Phase 01
Priority Mapping & Quick Win Audit

Ran a focused audit to identify the highest-leverage opportunities achievable within 3 months — prioritising pages with existing impressions but low CTR, categories with ranking potential, and structured data gaps.

Key Takeaways

What this project taught me.

01

Entity SEO is the fastest path to category authority. For niche product categories like luxury fragrance, building entity signals — terminology, attributes, semantic relationships — moves rankings faster than link building or generic content volume.

02

Short engagements demand ruthless prioritisation. 3 months is enough to move the needle significantly — if you choose the right 3 things. Most of the SEO work that gets done isn't the most impactful. The audit-first, priority-mapping approach makes every week count.

03

AI content scales product SEO without sacrificing quality. With the right prompting framework and editorial review, AI-assisted product descriptions can be entity-rich, unique, and conversion-friendly — at a speed and cost no traditional copywriting approach can match.
