How it works

Send me a brief via WhatsApp or email — the client's website, business goals, current SEO situation, and what you need delivered. No lengthy intake forms. If there's a scope document, pass it along; if not, I'll ask a few targeted questions and put one together.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, absolutely. A mutual NDA can be signed before any work begins — or even before the first call if you'd prefer. Confidentiality is standard practice, not an add-on.

Will my clients ever know you're involved?

Never. All deliverables carry your agency's name. I don't communicate with your clients, appear on calls with them, or have any visible presence in the project. You present the work as your own — because effectively it is.

Can I see samples of your work before starting?

Yes. You can review the case studies on this site, and I'm happy to share anonymised deliverable samples (audit reports, content briefs, strategy documents) on request during our initial conversation.

How do you handle revisions?

Revisions are included as part of the project scope. If a deliverable doesn't meet the agreed brief, I'll revise it until it does — no extra charge for reasonable amends. I aim to get it right the first time by asking the right questions upfront.

What formats do deliverables come in?

Typically Google Docs or Sheets for strategies, briefs, and audits — easy to copy into your own branded templates. Reports can be formatted in Google Slides or a PDF-ready layout with your logo applied. If you have a preferred format, just let me know.

Do you work with multiple agencies at the same time?

Yes, but I keep the roster intentionally small so every client gets focused attention. If you're on a monthly retainer, I'll allocate dedicated capacity so your projects are never deprioritised.

How do we communicate during a project?

WhatsApp for quick updates and questions, email for formal briefs and deliverable handoffs. I'll send status updates at key milestones so you're never chasing for an update.

Is there a minimum project size?

No hard minimum — I work on both one-off projects (a single audit, a content strategy) and ongoing monthly retainers. The best way to start is to send me a brief and I'll respond with a clear scope and price within 24 hours.
