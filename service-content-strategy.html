How it works

The content strategy process

01
Keyword Research & Clustering

Pulling your full keyword universe from GSC, Ahrefs, and Semrush, then running Python-based semantic clustering to group related terms. The output is a prioritised content calendar mapped to search volume and commercial intent.

02
Intent & Gap Analysis

Mapping your existing content against the full opportunity set — identifying gaps where competitors rank but you don't, and where your content is losing to better-structured pages. Reddit and Quora mining surfaces the language your audience actually uses.

03
Brief Creation & Content Production

Writer-ready briefs for every piece with target keywords, SERP intent, structure, schema requirements, and internal link targets. Content can be AI-assisted with human editing, or briefed to your existing writers — your choice.

04
Measure, Refresh & Iterate

Monthly tracking of content performance by cluster — impressions, clicks, and ranking movement. Underperforming content gets refreshed or restructured. High-potential pieces get schema upgrades and internal link boosts to accelerate ranking.
