The Core Stack

Built around Clay and n8n

🟦
Clay

The data layer. Clay pulls from 100+ enrichment sources — Apollo, LinkedIn, Clearbit, Hunter — to build the most complete picture of every lead before a single message is sent. Waterfall enrichment means you only pay for what you actually use.

🔀
n8n

The automation layer. n8n connects every tool in your GTM stack and executes the logic — trigger on signals, route leads, send personalised sequences, update your CRM, and loop back results for optimisation. No wasted manual effort.

🔗
Together

Clay handles the "who and what" — finding, validating, and enriching your exact ICP with buying signals, technographics, and intent data. n8n handles the "when and how" — automating every action in your GTM motion the moment a signal fires. The result is a self-running revenue engine that compounds month after month.

What you get

Everything built into your GTM engine

🎯
ICP Definition & Account Targeting

We map your ideal customer profile with precision — firmographics, technographics, job titles, and buying signals. Clay builds a continuously refreshed account list that only includes companies ready to buy.

🔍
Waterfall Lead Enrichment

Multi-source enrichment via Clay pulls verified emails, mobile numbers, LinkedIn data, and company intelligence — cascading through providers until every field is complete. No more bounced emails or blank CRM rows.

⚡
Signal-Based Triggers

Reach prospects at the exact moment they're likely to buy — job changes, funding rounds, LinkedIn activity, hiring signals, and technology installs. n8n fires the right sequence within hours of the signal, not weeks.

📨
Multi-Channel Outreach Automation

Coordinated sequences across email (Smartlead / Lemlist), LinkedIn, and direct mail — all triggered and managed by n8n. Every touchpoint is personalised using Clay-enriched variables, not mail merge.

🤖
AI-Powered Personalisation

GPT-4 integrated into Clay and n8n to write opening lines, tailor value propositions to each prospect's context, and adjust messaging based on their LinkedIn activity, recent news, or company milestones.

📊
Reporting & Continuous Optimisation

A live Looker Studio or Notion dashboard tracking reply rates, meeting booked rates, and pipeline generated per sequence. Monthly optimisation cycles improve what's working and kill what isn't.

How it works

The build process

01
Discovery & ICP Workshop

We start with a structured session to define your ICP precisely — the specific company types, roles, pain points, and buying triggers that represent your best-fit customers. This becomes the foundation every workflow is built on. Bad targeting is the single biggest GTM failure point, so we get this right first.

02
Data Infrastructure & Enrichment Setup

Clay tables are built with your ICP filters, enrichment waterfalls configured across Apollo, LinkedIn, Hunter, and Clearbit, and data validation rules applied to ensure only clean, verified leads enter the system. CRM integration is wired so nothing falls through the cracks.

03
Workflow & Sequence Build

n8n workflows are built for each signal type — job change, funding, intent spike, LinkedIn visit, and more. Each workflow triggers the right outreach sequence with AI-personalised messaging, routes responses to the right team member, and logs every interaction in your CRM automatically.

04
Launch, Monitor & Optimise

The engine goes live with a warm-up phase to protect sender reputation. Weekly monitoring tracks deliverability, reply rates, and meeting conversion. Monthly optimisation cycles refine targeting, update messaging, and expand signal coverage — the system improves as it learns.
